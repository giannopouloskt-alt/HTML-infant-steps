Who We Are

Discover Leafnet LTD: Pioneers in Online Learning Solutions

Since its establishment in 2007, Leafnet LTD has successfully transformed conventional educational programs into dynamic and interactive online curriculums, enhancing learner engagement and program effectiveness for numerous clients across various sectors.

Leafnet’s strategic investments in web-based training since 2015 have led to significant advancements in eLearning technologies, resulting in superior educational experiences and measurable outcomes for our partners.

Our Journey

A Timeline of Innovation and Growth

Founded in 2007 in Nicosia, Cyprus, Leafnet LTD has evolved over the years from a traditional education provider to a leader in online learning solutions. Our commitment to innovation took a leap in 2015 when we dedicated resources to enhance our web-based training methodologies, harnessing technologies that have since become integral in transforming educational experiences.

Starting as a conventional educational firm, Leafnet shifted its focus in 2015 to online learning, rapidly evolving to meet the needs of modern learners and establishing itself as a key player in the eLearning industry.
